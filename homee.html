What is PCOS/PMOS?

PCOS (Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome) also known as PMOS (Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome) is a hormonal disorder that affects women of reproductive age.

Hormonal Disorder

PCOS affects hormone levels and ovulation.

Common Condition

Affects about 1 in 10 women worldwide

Manageable

Healthy lifestyle and proper treatment can help.

Symptoms

Weight Gain

Gaining Weight even you're on a diet.

Irregular Periods

Either you're not getting your period for months or you're on your period for a month.

Acne

Heavy pimples even you're not on your period.

Excess Hair Growth

Hair Growth in your legs, armpits, mustache.

Treatment

Healthy Diet

Eat healthy foods, cut sugars, eat whole foods, high-fiber carbohydrates and lean proteins.

Exercise

More on Cardio. Jogging. Walking. Jumping Jacks.

Medication

Take Inositol(consult your doctor).

Regular Check-ups

Always consult with your Obgyne.
